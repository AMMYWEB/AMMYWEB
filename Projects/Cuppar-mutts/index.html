About Cupar Mutts

Established in 2012, Cupar Mutts provides professional, loving doggy day care from our base at Robertson Farm. Whether you work full-time or simply need a helping hand, we treat every dog as part of our family.

Our spacious grounds give dogs plenty of room to run, play, and socialise in a safe environment. We also specialise in helping breeders socialise puppies before they go to their forever homes.
